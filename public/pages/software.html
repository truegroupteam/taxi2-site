Three apps. One system.

Everything is live: the passenger orders, the nearest free car gets the order, the office sees it all on the map.

For the office

Admin panel

Works on a computer, tablet or phone. You see every car on the map and every order from start to finish.

  • Live map: free, on the way to a client, on a ride, busy
  • Zones and a queue in each zone, like a radio taxi
  • Orders, statistics, turnover by driver and by day
  • Drivers and cars: documents, contracts, sanctions, behaviour
  • Driver wallet: monthly fee and fee per order
  • Messages and push notifications, VIP clients, lost items
  • Admins with their own rights, every setting searchable
For the driver

Driver app

Made for a tablet in the car and for a phone. Big buttons, large text, nothing extra. Learned in minutes.

  • One tap to accept: address and distance
  • The zone queue: who is first
  • Navigation with Google Maps or Waze
  • Chat with the passenger without sharing numbers
  • Taximeter over Bluetooth: the fare comes in by itself
  • Day and night view, short breaks, own wallet
For the passenger

Passenger app

Ordering without a phone call. The passenger sees the price up front and the car on the map as it comes.

  • Estimated price by your own tariff
  • The car on the map and minutes to arrival
  • Chat with the driver, share the trip with family
  • Favourite places, ride history, driver rating
  • In Bulgarian and English
  • Opens in the browser, no app store needed

Why it works without a dispatcher

Orders go out by themselvesFirst to the cars in the passenger's zone in queue order, then to the nearest ones around. If nobody accepts, the order waits and any driver can take it.
Own mapsMaps, address search and routing run on our own server. No monthly Google bills per order.
Updates itselfThe apps open in the browser and are always the latest version. There is also an installable Android app.
Everything is set in the panelTariff, zones, rules, extras like estate car or 7 seats, texts. No programmer needed for each change.
Tested under loadTested with 150 cars online at the same time on one server.
Your data stays yoursEach company has its own separate system and database. Nightly backup.

Questions

Can it carry our name and logo?

Yes. The apps come with your name, logo and colours. This is done during setup.

Do drivers have to install an app?

Not necessarily. The app opens in the browser of the phone or tablet and can be added as an icon. There is also an installable Android app.

Does it work with our taximeters?

Elitax meters connect over Bluetooth and the fare comes in by itself. For another meter model ask us: it can be added as custom work.

What about our data?

It is yours only. Each company works in its own separate system with its own database. Other companies never see it.

Is the demo real?

Yes, it is the same software. Only the cars, drivers and passengers are made up, and everything is wiped every night.

Who is behind the software?

Taxi Labs, the developer of Two Taxi. You can also write to us through the developer form.
