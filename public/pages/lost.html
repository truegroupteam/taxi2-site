Lost items

Left something in a taxi?

Tell us right away. The driver checks the car and you agree how to get it back.

In the app — the fastest way

Up to 72 hours after the ride. The driver is notified at once.

  1. Tap “Left something”Open “Rides” in the menu and choose the ride. The button is also on the screen right after the ride.
  2. The driver checks the carIf the item is there, the driver writes to you in the app, maybe with a photo.
  3. Choose how to get itGo to the car for free, or the driver brings it to you. Bringing it is paid by the taximeter, and you see the estimate before you decide.

Please note: Two Taxi is not responsible for lost items. The driver will check the car, but the item may have been left somewhere else.
