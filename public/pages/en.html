How to order

Three steps. No need to explain where you are.

  1. 1Choose where toThe app knows where you are. You see the distance and the estimated price.
  2. 2We find a carThe order goes to the nearest free car. No dispatcher.
  3. 3The car comesYou see it on the map, its plate, model and in how many minutes it arrives.

No app store needed. It opens in your phone’s browser, and you can add it to your home screen as an icon.

Questions

Do I need to download an app?

No. The app opens in your phone’s browser. You can add it to your home screen and then it opens like any other app.

How much does a ride cost?

By the fares above. You pay what the taximeter in the car shows. Before you order, the app shows an estimated price.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, to the driver in the car. Cash is fine too.

When does it start?

Soon in Plovdiv. Leave a phone number or e-mail and we will write to you once when it is ready.

I left something in the taxi. What do I do?

In the app, open “Rides”, choose the ride and tap “Left something” (up to 72 hours after the ride). No app, or lost your phone? Use the lost items form.
